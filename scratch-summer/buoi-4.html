Goals

What are we making today?

The whole studio builds Hạ Long Dodge — a sailboat drifts through the bay while rocks keep drifting in. 3 lives. Hit a rock, lose a life. Run out of lives, it's game over. The longer you survive, the higher your score.
⛵ 🪨 ❤️ 💥
  • Manage two variables at once: Score and Lives
  • Build a Game Over screen with broadcast + stop all
  • Learn the design rule: a good game gets harder little by little
  • End of session: survival contest — who lasts longest on someone else's game?
🎮 Warm-up (10 min)

What's been missing from every game so far?

🌊 30 seconds in Hạ Long Bay

Today's setting — towering limestone rocks rising out of the water. For some of you, this might be the very first time you've seen it.

Design question: think back to the last 3 games we built. What's missing? You can't lose. A game that can't be lost gives you nothing to fight for.
Today we fix that — a boat, some rocks, and 3 lives. Suddenly every move matters.
Key words · Từ khóa hôm nay

6 words we'll use all session 🇬🇧 🇻🇳

Lives Mạng — how many hits you can take before it's over.
Game over Kết thúc game — the screen that shows when your lives hit 0.
Speed Tốc độ — how fast something moves across the screen.
Difficulty Độ khó — how hard the game feels to play.
Show / Hide Hiện / Ẩn — make a sprite appear or disappear.
Stop all Dừng tất cả — freeze every script the moment the game ends.

New knowledge (15 min)

The 3 pieces of a "you can lose" game

❤️ Lives = 3 Hit a rock → change Lives by -1. Same variable trick as Score, just counting down instead of up.
📢 broadcast if Lives = 0 then broadcast GameOver — one sprite shouts a message, any other sprite can listen for it.
🛑 show + stop all The GAME OVER text sprite hears the broadcast → show + stop all. Every script in the project freezes at once.
💡 Broadcast is the most abstract idea in the whole camp — one sprite doesn't know or care who's listening, it just shouts. Today we use it exactly once, for Game Over.
🤔 Predict: if we forget to hide the GAME OVER text at the very start, what does the screen look like the moment you hit the green flag?

Level up · 🟡 Normal

NORMAL 🛟 A life buoy drifts by

Every so often a life buoy floats across the bay. Catch it and get an extra life — miss it, and it's gone.

Requirements

  • A separate life buoy sprite, hidden most of the time
  • Appears rarely — wait a random 8–15 seconds between appearances
  • Drifts across like a rock, but touching it → change Lives by 1
  • A cheerful sound when caught, then hide

🕹️ Playtest (10 min)

Survival contest — who lasts longest?

Swap devices. Everyone plays a friend's game once — whoever survives the longest on that game wins the round.

📈 Compare the ramp-up

Whose game feels like it gets harder little by little, and whose feels like it jumps straight to "impossible"? That difference is real game design.
